## Story

July 2024 we spent two weeks in Boston crashing with our friends Samuel and Jessica and their kids Elijah and Ella. Most of the trip we were out in Plymouth - pool time, and a serious commitment to the local ice cream scene. Far Far's was a favorite, plus a couple others whose names I've already lost. We also made it to the ocean, which felt like a requirement once you're that close to the water.

We still carved out a couple nights downtown. Walked past the Boston Marathon finish line on Boylston, then met Zach and Claire in the North End for dinner.
