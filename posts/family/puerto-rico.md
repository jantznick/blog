## Story

We landed in Aguadilla, grabbed an Airbnb, and spent most of our time bouncing between beaches. West-side PR is quieter than San Juan - more local rhythm, less cruise-ship density.

Eventually we drove east toward San Juan, with a swimming stop at Manantial Ojo de Agua. We also made it into El Yunque and did the Las Paylas rock water slide, which the kids (and honestly the adults) thought was excellent. In the capital we caught a Gigantes beisbol game, walked Old San Juan, and watched the kids lose their minds over poppers. Then home.
