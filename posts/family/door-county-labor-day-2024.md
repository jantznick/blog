## Story

Labor Day weekend 2024 we rented a camper van and pointed it toward Door County. Base camp was Baileys Harbor.

Beach day was Peninsula State Park. We hit Plum Loco for the petting zoo, a mini golf place where the kids mined for rocks, and a bounce house. One night we drove up to Sister Bay for dinner. Another highlight: PC Junction, where your food arrives by train - the kids loved that.
